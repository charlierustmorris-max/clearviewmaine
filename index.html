Terms of Service

1. Services

Clear View Maine provides residential window washing and related cleaning services in Yarmouth, Maine and surrounding areas. All services are performed by appointment only. The scope of each job is confirmed in writing via a quote prior to commencement of work.

2. Quotes & Pricing

Quotes are provided at no charge following an on-site assessment. Pricing is based on window count, type, and service tier as outlined on this site. Final pricing is confirmed in writing before work begins and will not change unless additional work is requested by the customer.

Appointments may be cancelled or rescheduled with at least 24 hours' notice at no cost. Cancellations made with less than 24 hours' notice may be subject to a rescheduling fee at our discretion.
